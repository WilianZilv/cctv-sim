# Reference data (condensed)

E = estimate, V = verified with a source.

## Streets
- Local carriageway 7-9 m between curbs (E); lanes 2.8-3.2 m; parallel parking 2.0-2.2 m, 60-80% occupied in residential areas (E).
- SP sidewalk: faixa livre >= 1.20 m, faixa de servico 0.70 m, guia 0.15 m wide (V, Decreto 59.671/2020). Total 2-3 m (E).
- Curb 0.12-0.15 m; sarjeta 0.30-0.50 m (E).
- Lots: 10x25 classic SP, 5x25 / 6x25 older areas; Curitiba 12x30 (E). Front setback 5 m where required, many "recuo zero" garages.
- Front walls 1.8-2.5 m: solid base + grille, or full plastered wall with electric fence / concertina. Gates: 1.0 m pedestrian, 3.0-3.5 m vehicle.

## Surfaces
- CBUQ asphalt, oxidizes to light gray; 15-30% patched/damaged area (E).
- SP sidewalks: concrete ~45%, ladrilho/caquinho ~15%, paver ~15%, mixed/cimento queimado ~15%, grass ~10% (E). Per-lot level changes and driveway ramps.
- Curitiba: petit-pave basalt + white stone patterns, concrete elsewhere.
- Tactile paving yellow/red. Curbs painted yellow (no stopping) or whitewashed.

## Street furniture
- Concrete poles 9-12 m (duplo T in SP), spacing 30-40 m. Primary 9-10 m, secondary 6-7 m, telecom 4.5-6 m. Transformers every few hundred meters.
- Streetlights: mostly LED 4000-5000 K at 8-9 m on 1.5-2 m arms; leftover HPS sodium (2000 K orange).
- Curitiba elevated trash cages; SP wheeled bins / black bags on the curb.
- Lombadas yellow/black stripes ~3.7 m wide, 0.1 m tall.

## Fleet
- 2025 top sellers: Strada, Polo, Argo, HB20, Onix, Mobi (V). Old fleet ~10 years: Gol, Palio, Uno, Celta, Fox, Ka, Sandero, Corolla, Civic.
- Body: hatch 50-55%, sedan 20-25%, SUV 12-18%, pickup/van 5-8% (E).
- Colors in circulation: white ~30, silver ~22, black ~22, gray ~15, other ~11 (E).
- Motorcycles 25-30% of flow (E); delivery boxes red/orange/black.

## CCTV
- Intelbras VHD 1220 B: 1080p 1/2.7", 3.6 mm, HFOV 92-98 deg (V). VHD 1220 D: 2.8 mm 109 deg (V). IR 20 m, 850 nm.
- DVR 1080N/1080 lite at 12-15 fps, 1-2 Mbps H.264 (E). Blocking in dark/foliage, smearing on motion.
- Mount 2.5-3.5 m, tilted 15-30 deg down.
- IR cut switch at 5-10 lux: grayscale, center hotspot, plates blown out, insects near LEDs.

## Weather
- Curitiba 945 m altitude, fog ~10 days/month in June-July, clears ~9h; rain on ~43% of days.
- SP: sunrise 05:25-06:45, sunset 17:30-18:50 (UTC-3, no DST). 40-50% cloudy days, summer afternoon storms.
